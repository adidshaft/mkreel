# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of different viewpoints and constraints.
- Give clear, constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when needed.
- Focus on what helps the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or maintainer workflows.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; temporarily limit participation; or ban contributors when behavior violates this code.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact paths listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Maintainers will review reports in good faith and avoid unnecessary disclosure.
